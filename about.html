Who Are BRAG?

Bully Rescue & Advocacy Group envisions a world where all pets are free from abuse, negelect, and discrimination

We are an all volunteer rescue & advocacy group whos mission is to rescue animals who have been abused, abandoned, or otherwise destined to be euthanized. We are an all-breed rescue with a soft spot in our hearts for the misunderstood bully breed dogs. Our goal is to help educate others and erase the negative stigma associated with "Pit Bull" dogs. By working with responsible foster homes, we are able to provide our dogs with a loving environment. These environments include being kept as a family pet, being socialized, and trained on the basics like manners and crate training. Through donations, all animals rescued by BRAG are spayed and neutered, vaccinated, and implanted with a microchip before being adopted into a well matched and carefully screened adoptive home. We work with responsible owners who need assistance with food, routine care, and training.

Our Beliefs:

  • We believe in the No Kill Equation
  • We believe dogs are individuals and should not be labeled
  • We believe that all dogs deserve a first, second, third, and more chances at living the best life that they possibly can live
  • We believe that there is no such thing as a bad dog, only bad owners
  • We believe that all animals need to be in an environment to fit their specific needs and personality
  • We believe that dogs are intended to be family pets and need to be placed accordingly
  • We believe that when people are considering pet ownership, they should consider adoption from a rescue or shelter rather than considering for-profit breeders or puppy mills
