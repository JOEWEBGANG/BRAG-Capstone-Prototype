About BRAG'S Adoption Process

The adoption process can take anywhere from one to several weeks. Please be patient as we volunteer our time and with the large number of animals in need we may not be able to respond the same day you apply. We do our best to respond to all emails in a timely manner however!

Once we have your completed application, which can be found under the forms tab, our adoption coordinator will schedule a phone interview with you. Next, we will speak with the references you provided and your current vetrinarian to verify that all animals in your home are up to date on vaccinations and preventatives.

Renters, please verify with your landlord or management company that you are allowed to have a dog AND that there are no breed restrictions prior to submitting your application!

The final step will be a home visit from a BRAG volunteer. We love and care for these dogs as if they are our own and want to be sure that they are going to be well cared for in a safe environment!

All BRAG dogs and puppies are vaccinated for distemper, parvo, and rabies. In addition they are spayed or neutered and implanted with a microchip. BRAG dogs and puppies are also kept up to date on heartworm, flea, and tick prevention.

Your adoption also includes 30 days of pet insurance.

We strongly encourage you to do research and to be proactive with training from day one! Please consult the resources on our home page for helpful tips!
